What should I buy?

Gun Guide

A starting-point buying reference for new and entry-level owners. Four budget tiers, seven kinds of gun, two picks in each — with the cartridge it feeds on. Pick a tier up top.

Read first. This is a plain-language starting point, not legal, safety, or investment advice. Prices are rough U.S. street estimates as of late 2025 and move constantly with the market — treat them as ballpark, not quotes. Fit, intended use, and your local laws matter more than any list here. Always buy through a licensed dealer, follow all federal, state, and local law, and get hands-on training before you carry or rely on any firearm.
